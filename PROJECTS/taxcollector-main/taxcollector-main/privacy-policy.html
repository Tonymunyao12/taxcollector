Your privacy matters

Privacy policy.

A clear explanation of how Tax Collector handles information shared through this website.

Last updated: 22 September 2026

Information we collect

When you use our quote form, you may provide your name, phone number, selected service, and any details you choose to include. If you contact us by email, phone, or WhatsApp, we receive the information you share in that conversation.

How we use your information

We use your information to respond to enquiries, prepare or discuss service quotes, provide requested tax and registration support, and communicate about your request. We do not sell your personal information.

WhatsApp enquiries

Submitting the quote form opens WhatsApp with a prepared message. You choose whether to send it. Once sent, the information is also handled according to WhatsApp’s own privacy practices.

Keeping information secure

We take reasonable steps to keep enquiry information private and only share it when needed to provide the requested service or when required by law.

Your choices

You can ask what personal information we hold about your enquiry, request a correction, or ask us to stop contacting you. Email hello@taxcollector.co.ke with your request.

Questions

For privacy questions, contact us through our Contact page.
